# RUNDOWN — Gemini / Veo Video Prompts

Prompts for **Google Veo 3** (in Gemini / Flow / Vertex). Two hero videos:
**A) "Become the Running Man" hype/recruitment ad** and **B) Hunter-POV AR chase (see it through your phone).**
Plus bonus variants.

### How to use these (read first)
- **Veo clips are ~8 seconds.** For a full ad, generate each numbered **SHOT** separately and edit
  them together, or use Flow's "extend/scene builder" to chain them. Keep the same style line in
  every shot for consistency.
- **Audio:** Veo 3 generates sound + dialogue. Dialogue in quotes renders as spoken lines; keep
  lines short. Prefix ambient/music with "Audio:".
- **On-screen text/HUD/logos are unreliable in AI video** — generate the footage clean and add the
  reticle, "$100,000", "LIVE", the RUNDOWN wordmark, and captions in post (After Effects/CapCut).
- **The runner look (keep identical across shots):** a **muscular male figure made of glowing
  neon-green light (#00FF6A)**, translucent hologram with a bright emissive rim, soft green bloom,
  faint cyan edge light, motion-trail streaks behind him, subtle horizontal scanline shimmer —
  clearly an **AR overlay**, not a real person. Night city.
- **Aspect ratio:** use **16:9** for the ad, **9:16 vertical** for social / the POV chase / pump.fun.
- Add this **negative prompt** where offered: `text, watermark, logo, subtitles, distorted hands,
  extra limbs, cartoonish, low quality, daytime`.

## A) "BECOME THE RUNNING MAN" — hype / recruitment ad (16:9)

> Cinematic, dystopian-game-show energy meets crypto. Movie-grade. Generate SHOT 1→5, cut together
> to ~30–40s. Consistent style line included in each.

**STYLE LINE (paste in every shot):** *Cinematic dystopian sci-fi ad, anamorphic lens, high
contrast neon-noir, rain-slicked night city, teal-and-magenta color grade with electric-green
accents, film grain, shallow depth of field, dramatic volumetric lighting, premium commercial look.*

**SHOT 1 — Cold open (8s)**
```
A dark, rain-slicked city street at night, empty and silent, neon signs reflecting in puddles.
Slow cinematic push-in down the street. Sudden electric-green glitch flickers across the frame.
[STYLE LINE]. Audio: low ominous drone, distant siren, a single deep bass hit on the glitch.
Mood: tense, foreboding, "something is coming."
```

**SHOT 2 — The reveal (8s)**
```
Through the same night street, a MUSCULAR MALE FIGURE MADE OF GLOWING NEON-GREEN LIGHT — a
translucent hologram with bright emissive edges, green bloom, cyan rim light, and motion-trail
streaks — suddenly SPRINTS across the frame from left to right and vanishes around a corner.
Fast whip-pan following him, then he's gone. [STYLE LINE]. Audio: a sharp whoosh, pounding
footsteps turning into a heartbeat-like bass pulse, an announcer whisper "He's running."
```

**SHOT 3 — The world watches (8s)**
```
Rapid cinematic montage: diverse people in different cities at night raising glowing phones toward
empty streets; on each phone screen the neon-green running figure appears in augmented reality
while the real street behind the phone is empty. Crowds, rooftops, alleys. Energy building.
[STYLE LINE]. Audio: rising electronic pulse, crowd murmurs, rhythmic percussion accelerating.
```

**SHOT 4 — The stakes (8s)**
```
Cinematic close-ups: a determined hunter's eyes lit green by a phone screen; a targeting reticle
locking (leave space for HUD in post); stacks of glowing digital coins/tokens swirling; the
neon-green runner glancing back over his shoulder mid-sprint, defiant, a confident half-smile.
[STYLE LINE]. Audio: heavy cinematic braams, a ticking clock, an announcer: "One runner. A whole
city hunting. Real money on the line."
```

**SHOT 5 — Call to action (8s)**
```
The neon-green runner bursts toward camera and dissolves into a shower of green particles that
swirl and reform into empty black space (leave clean center for the RUNDOWN logo in post).
Final slow particle drift. [STYLE LINE]. Audio: one last big bass hit then near-silence, a low
voice: "RUNDOWN. Become part of the hunt." Subtle synth sting to end.
```

## B) HUNTER-POV — "SEE IT THROUGH YOUR PHONE" (9:16 vertical)

> First-person, hand-held, social-native. The killer idea: the naked-eye street is empty, but
> **through the raised phone the glowing runner appears**. Great for TikTok/Reels/pump.fun.

**SHOT 1 — The reveal in AR (8s)**
```

figure made of glowing neon-green holographic light is standing 20 meters down the street, then
turns and STARTS TO RUN. Handheld camera shake, realistic phone screen with the AR figure
composited into the live street. Wet asphalt, neon reflections, breath visible in cold air.
Cinematic, photoreal, night. Audio: heavy breathing, ambient city hum, a synth "ping" as the
figure is detected, quickening footsteps. Negative prompt: text, watermark, distorted hands,
```

## Consistency & production tips
- **Lock the character:** reuse the exact runner description (muscular, neon-green holographic,
  emissive rim, motion streaks, scanlines) verbatim in every prompt. If your tool supports a
  **seed** or **reference image**, feed it a frame from the best clip (or your
  `rundown-hologram.png` / a still from `rundown-visual-mock.html`) to keep him identical.
- **Do HUD + branding in post:** reticle, "$100,000", "LIVE", transport-mode chip, RUNDOWN
  wordmark, captions — overlay in CapCut/Premiere/After Effects using the brand colors
  (green #00FF6A, cyan #00E5FF, magenta #FF2E9A, amber #FFB020).
- **Grade to match the mock:** push blacks, add green glow/bloom, subtle scanlines and film grain
  so the videos match `rundown-visual-mock.html` and the design system.
- **Music:** dark, driving electronic / cinematic trailer beat; sidechain to the bass hits.
- If a clip renders the runner as a *real* man (not holographic), add to the prompt:
  *"clearly a translucent glowing hologram made of light, not a real solid person, semi-transparent,
  emissive."*
